The shift

AI capability is advancing quickly. Human experience is now the differentiator.

The next generation of AI products will be judged by more than technical performance. They will be judged by whether people understand them, trust them, find value in them, and choose to keep using them.

MindPort helps teams building at the edge of AI answer the human questions that shape product success.

How do people interpret what the system can do?

Where does trust break down?

What makes an AI interaction worth returning to?

Which product decisions will drive real adoption?

What we do

Human–AI interaction research and strategy.

We study how people interact with AI systems, products, and experiences — then translate that understanding into practical direction for product and strategy teams.

  • Understand how users experience AI products and features
  • Identify barriers to trust, comprehension, usefulness, and adoption
  • Validate new product concepts, interaction models, and experience directions
  • Improve existing AI products through research-led recommendations
  • Define product roadmaps around the human behaviors that matter most
  • Explore emerging interaction patterns across generative AI, agents, multimodal tools, autonomous systems, wearables, robotics, and embedded AI
Who we work with

AI-native companies and the product teams shaping what comes next.

That includes teams developing generative tools, agentic products, search and discovery systems, creative platforms, autonomous experiences, wearables, robotics, embedded AI, and other emerging technologies where human interaction is central to success.

We also partner with enterprise product and innovation teams building AI-enabled products, services, and customer or employee experiences.

AIX

Artificial Intelligence Experience.

AIX is MindPort's framework for understanding how humans experience AI systems. It combines UX, HCI, product research, behavioral science, psychology, and social research to examine the dimensions that shape successful AI products.

Comprehension. Trust. Control. Usefulness. Interaction. Adoption. Meaning.

AIX helps teams move beyond technical capability to understand the experience conditions that determine whether an AI product becomes useful, trusted, and valued.
